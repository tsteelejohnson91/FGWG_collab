How the links were counted

Counts are distinct PubMed-indexed papers across all years, found by author-pair searches (5 and 16 October 2026) and checked by affiliation to exclude namesakes. Preprints later published, author corrections and retracted papers are left out; preprints and abstracts not yet published are shown separately and marked. Conference-only venues and arXiv are not covered, so links for the computer-science members are lower bounds.

A link means shared authorship, not necessarily a joint project. Several single-paper links are large multi-author or consortium papers; the "hide single-paper links" option removes them. Theme assignments are provisional and based on publication records. Yunlong Liu (26 links) and Kun Huang (10 links, 126 shared papers) sit at the center as the hubs of the center's two largest communities; the line between them is their own 7 shared papers.
