How the links were counted

Counts are distinct PubMed-indexed papers across all years, found by author-pair searches on 5 October 2026 and checked by affiliation. Preprints later published, author corrections and one retracted paper are left out. Preprints not yet published are listed separately and marked.

A link means shared authorship, not necessarily a joint project. Several single-paper links are large multi-author studies, and most Liu–Wan papers are core-supported collaborations. Shared grants, unpublished work and papers outside PubMed are not captured.

Node color and ring position follow each person's primary theme, which is a provisional assignment. Yunlong Liu sits at the center because he links to 11 of the other 17 members.
